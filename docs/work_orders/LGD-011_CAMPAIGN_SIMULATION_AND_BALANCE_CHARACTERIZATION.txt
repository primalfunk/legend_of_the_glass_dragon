LEGEND OF THE GLASS DRAGON
WORK ORDER LGD-011
CAMPAIGN SIMULATION + BALANCE CHARACTERIZATION

STATUS

AUTHORIZED FOR IMPLEMENTATION

MISSION

Measure Legend of the Glass Dragon as a complete game using deterministic,
non-human simulation. Build or extend development-only tooling that starts
fresh seasons, drives lawful production-domain actions, advances the simulated
community and Dragon Season, reaches campaign conclusion, and records
structured metrics.

PHASE BOUNDARY

This work order is measurement, simulation, characterization and objective
defect discovery. It is not discretionary balance tuning. The Principal, not
the Builder, decides whether measured balance matches the desired experience.

No human playtesting is requested or permitted in this phase.

REQUIRED POLICIES

- Casual / Conservative;
- Ordinary;
- Aggressive / Optimizing;
- Reckless;
- Passive.

Policy rules must be deterministic, bounded, transparent and documented. The
harness must use ordinary production game rules wherever practical rather than
an alternate toy simulation.

QUALIFICATION SAMPLE

Attempt at least 1,000 complete seasons, preferably 2,500–5,000 when practical,
with a large Ordinary cohort and varied deterministic seeds. Apply a generous
100-game-day ceiling and classify any campaign beyond it as unresolved rather
than forcing victory.

MEASUREMENT SCOPE

Characterize distributions—not only averages—for:

- campaign duration and termination;
- player/NPC Dragon victories and attempts;
- player level timing and final level;
- Physical Training attempts and failures;
- forest combat, enemies and daily fight use;
- economy, banking, healing, defeat losses and equipment;
- NPC progression and equipment;
- player rankings and ranking churn;
- Dragon readiness and stage timing;
- society activity, Chronicle density, callbacks and NPC spotlight;
- policy differences, confidence/sample stability and reproducible outliers.

Preserve per-season and per-day machine-readable evidence plus enemy aggregates,
summary distributions and outlier seeds. Produce a human-readable Principal
decision report with compact tables and explicit numerical answers to the 20
questions in the authorized work order.

DETERMINISM AND SAVE/RESTORE

Rerun representative seeds for every policy and require identical authoritative
outcomes. Compare bounded continuous campaigns with campaigns encoded/decoded
at selected day boundaries. A restart must not change the world outcome.

OBJECTIVE DEFECT POLICY

Only objective correctness defects—nontermination, invalid state, duplicated
resolution, progression lock, overflow, unreachable lawful systems, and similar
failures—may be repaired. Preserve failing seeds and rerun the cohort after any
material production fix. Do not tune a valid distribution to personal taste.

OUT OF SCOPE

No human playtesting, factions, new gameplay, new Dragon mechanics, content,
audio, visual redesign, achievements, meta-progression or unauthorized balance
tuning.

COMPLETION STATE

LGD011_BALANCE_CHARACTERIZED_PRINCIPAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED

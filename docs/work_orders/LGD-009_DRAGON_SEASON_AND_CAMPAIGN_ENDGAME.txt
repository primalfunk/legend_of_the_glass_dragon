LGD-009 — DRAGON SEASON + CAMPAIGN ENDGAME

STATUS

AUTHORIZED FOR IMPLEMENTATION

MISSION

Turn Legend of the Glass Dragon from an indefinitely repeating fantasy life
simulation into a finite campaign with mounting pressure, competing ambitions,
and a meaningful conclusion.

The Glass Dragon becomes an active force. The campaign moves from ordinary
adventuring through rumor, concern, preparation, rivalry, crisis,
confrontation, and historical conclusion. The player matters but is not the
only resident capable of resolving the world.

======================================================================
1. CORE DESIGN PRINCIPLE
======================================================================

The Glass Dragon is the campaign-scale problem shared by the simulated
community. Residents hear rumors, react, prepare, fear, compete, improve,
acquire equipment, discuss candidates, help or obstruct rivals where history
justifies it, and may become challengers themselves. Pressure must appear in
Chronicle, Dawn Brief, gossip, rankings, NPC behavior, economy, relationships,
challenges, and story incidents. Do not reveal a simple boss button at a level.

======================================================================
2. THE SEASON
======================================================================

A new game is one finite season. Target meaningful early days, visible middle
pressure, late preparation/competition, and resolution roughly during Days
15–30 in a healthy campaign. Tune through play rather than imposing a rigid
calendar. Strong, weak, aggressive, and cautious play must remain coherent.

======================================================================
3. DRAGON PRESSURE
======================================================================

Persist authoritative Dragon pressure and explicit player-facing crisis stages:

STAGE 0 — WHISPERS
STAGE 1 — SIGNS
STAGE 2 — FEAR
STAGE 3 — CRISIS
STAGE 4 — CHALLENGE WINDOW
STAGE 5 — RESOLVED

Each stage escalates rumor, incidents, community response, champion discussion,
and confrontation availability. Names may be refined, but escalation must be
legible.

======================================================================
4. PRESSURE ADVANCEMENT
======================================================================

Advance pressure from game days, campaign state, major incidents, community
strength, and earlier Dragon events with controlled deterministic variation.
Narrative template choices must not perturb campaign outcomes.

======================================================================
5. DRAGON INCIDENTS
======================================================================

Create bounded stage-appropriate incidents: sightings, burned woodland,
missing travelers, survivors, destroyed property, glass fragments, nervous
merchants, reconnaissance, failed approaches, and declarations. Early events
are ambiguous; late events unmistakable. Do not mechanically spam one per day.

======================================================================
6. GLASS MOTIF
======================================================================

Use concise BBS-compatible crystalline scales, translucent fragments, mirrored
wounds, refracted light, glass residue, chiming, and splintered surfaces. Keep
the Dragon mysterious.

======================================================================
7. NPC AWARENESS
======================================================================

Resident response derives from personality, strength, wealth, relationships,
and memory. Bounded tendencies may include fearful, cautious, opportunistic,
supportive, ambitious, and obsessed. Behavior matters more than enum labels.

======================================================================
8. CHAMPION CANDIDATES
======================================================================

Derive plausible candidates from actual level, combat capability, equipment,
health, resources, victories, personality/confidence, rank/reputation, and
Dragon history. Several candidates may exist. Do not automatically select the
player or highest-level NPC.

======================================================================
9. PLAYER QUALIFICATION
======================================================================

Use understandable broad readiness based on level, combat strength, health,
equipment, and campaign stage. Explain missing readiness plainly rather than
exposing opaque formulas or requiring one exact build/day.

======================================================================
10. NPC QUALIFICATION
======================================================================

NPCs use comparable readiness, ordinary training, canonical equipment, wealth,
and recovery. They must become ready without cheating.

======================================================================
11. DECLARING INTENT
======================================================================

Late-game candidates may publicly or privately declare intent. Declarations
create real Chronicle/gossip/Dawn and relationship consequences—encouragement,
mockery, jealousy, fear, rivalry, help, or opposition—through existing systems.

======================================================================
12. PLAYER RESPONSE TO OTHER CHAMPIONS
======================================================================

Support competing through ordinary progression/challenges, supporting a
candidate through modest existing social/economic means, obstructing through
existing rivalry systems, and ignoring the crisis. Do not build a giant
companion system or a cartoon sabotage control. The world continues without the
player.

======================================================================
13. NPC DRAGON ATTEMPTS
======================================================================

Ready NPCs with appropriate disposition and campaign conditions may make
deterministic Dragon attempts. Results—decisive defeat, narrow defeat,
retreat/survival, or victory—must derive from actual state, not narrative coin
flips.

======================================================================
14. CONSEQUENCES OF NPC FAILURE
======================================================================

Failure may cause severe injury, money loss, supported equipment consequences,
reputation/relationship changes, recovery, and retry delay. Avoid casual
permadeath. Every attempt is major Chronicle material and applies once.

======================================================================
15. CONSEQUENCES OF NPC VICTORY
======================================================================

An NPC can legally slay the Dragon. The crisis ends, that resident becomes the
season victor, history records it, and the campaign concludes. The result must
be surprising but fair and visibly foreshadowed.

======================================================================
16. PLAYER DRAGON ATTEMPT
======================================================================

Provide a clear established-UI route when the challenge window is open and the
player is ready. Use strong ANSI event treatment and do not hide the culmination
behind obscure menu knowledge.

======================================================================
17. GLASS DRAGON COMBAT
======================================================================

Reuse existing combat architecture. Give the Dragon 2–3 bounded distinct
mechanics such as high health/damage, a defensive phase, a distinctive attack,
or resistance/weakness, with stronger text. Do not build a cinematic combat
engine.

======================================================================
18. FAIRNESS
======================================================================

The fight is dangerous but understandable. Readiness communication should let a
well-prepared player judge the risk. Loss comes from visible danger, not a hidden
arbitrary punishment.

======================================================================
19. RETRY RULES
======================================================================

A failed challenge requires recovery and cannot be repeated trivially the same
day. Prevent heal-and-spam loops.

======================================================================
20. DRAGON VICTORY
======================================================================

Player victory resolves combat, marks the crisis resolved, stores the player as
victor, creates a major structured Chronicle fact, preserves world reactions,
and enters conclusion without erasing accumulated state.

======================================================================
21. SEASON CONCLUSION
======================================================================

Create a concise campaign-specific sequence:

THE GLASS DRAGON HAS FALLEN (or alternate outcome)
→ FINAL GLASS CHRONICLE
→ SEASON RECORD
→ future-menu/completed-game boundary

Reflect victor, final player level/rank/wealth/equipment, strongest residents,
rivals and relationships, challenges, important events/attempts, and campaign
length without a giant statistics dump.

======================================================================
22. FINAL GLASS CHRONICLE
======================================================================

Generate a short historical account from real stored facts: season duration,
actual attempts, actual leaders/rivals, victor, and resolution day. Never
fabricate names, deeds, or relationships.

======================================================================
23. NON-PLAYER VICTORY CONCLUSION
======================================================================

Give NPC victory full legitimacy. Still summarize the player's real story—rank,
wealth, rivalry, support, opposition, or ordinary life. Do not frame every NPC
victory as player failure.

======================================================================
24. PLAYER NEVER CHALLENGED
======================================================================

The season may and must be able to end without a player attempt. Escalating
pressure and NPC agency prevent permanent waiting.

======================================================================
25. DEADLOCK PREVENTION
======================================================================

Prove campaigns cannot stall because nobody qualifies, candidates refuse,
economy blocks progress, attempts fail forever, pressure stalls, scheduling
never occurs, or the player must act. Use bounded, deterministic escalation while
remaining simulation-driven.

======================================================================
26. ECONOMY INTERACTION
======================================================================

Allow modest existing-economy preparation: equipment desirability and resident
investment. Do not add inflation, dynamic commodity markets, or scarcity
simulation.

======================================================================
27. RELATIONSHIP INTERACTION
======================================================================

Dragon ambition may cause justified envy, admiration, fear, rivalry, support,
resentment, or respect based on actual behavior/history. Do not globally mutate
every relationship at stage changes.

======================================================================
28. CHRONICLE INTEGRATION
======================================================================

LGD-008 remains authoritative. Dragon Season emits structured sightings,
incidents, intent, attempts, failure, and victory facts. Chronicle may know more
than Dawn.

======================================================================
29. INN GOSSIP INTEGRATION
======================================================================

Gossip imperfectly interprets public facts, intentions, champions, and rivalries.
It may exaggerate opinion but cannot contradict authoritative facts.

======================================================================
30. MAIN MENU BOUNDARY
======================================================================

Do not implement a new full Main Menu. Represent completed state cleanly for a
future New Game/completed-campaign flow and never destroy the season
automatically.

======================================================================
31. SAVE FORMAT
======================================================================

Persist Dragon pressure/stage, incidents, authoritative readiness as needed,
declared challengers, attempts, outcome, victor, resolution/conclusion state, and
final-summary inputs. Migrate existing saves into a sensible campaign state.

======================================================================
32. DETERMINISM
======================================================================

Identical starting state, seed, and player actions produce the same stage
history, candidates, decisions, attempts, outcomes, victor, and historical
facts. UI composition must never consume gameplay RNG.

======================================================================
33. ONE-DAY / MULTI-DAY PLAY
======================================================================

Campaign progression uses game days only and must work when one, twelve, or the
entire season is played in one physical sitting. Never use wall-clock waiting.

======================================================================
34. PLAYER COPY
======================================================================

Use direct energetic language accessible to a bright middle-school-aged player.
Keep internal simulation terminology out of player-facing copy.

======================================================================
35. VISUAL DIRECTION
======================================================================

Use LGD-007's ANSI identity. Escalate from cyan/white/gray through gold/magenta
to red/bright white/cyan crystal accents. Make the confrontation one of the
richest ANSI moments without switching to modern painted fantasy art.

======================================================================
36. AUDIO BOUNDARY
======================================================================

Continue the existing shuffle and universal click. Add no boss music, roar,
combat effects, victory fanfare, or crisis ambience.

======================================================================
37. BALANCE TARGET
======================================================================

Healthy campaigns produce several plausible adventurers, ranking change,
recognizable rivalries, multiple Dragon developments, often multiple late
candidates, a dangerous achievable player fight, and eventual resolution. NPC
victory must be neither irrelevant nor so common the player lacks a chance.

======================================================================
38. SIMULATION QUALIFICATION
======================================================================

Run at least 25 deterministic accelerated seasons. Record campaign length,
victor, player/NPC result for simulated policy, challengers, failed attempts,
strongest levels, deadlocks, and suspiciously identical patterns. Zero seasons
may deadlock.

======================================================================
39. PLAYER QUALIFICATION
======================================================================

Perform normal, aggressive, passive, marginal/underprepared player-defeat, and
deterministic NPC-victory cases. Inspect escalation, pacing, recovery, and full
conclusions.

======================================================================
40. TARGETED AUTOMATED TESTS
======================================================================

Prove deterministic/non-regressing stages, stage-valid incidents,
authoritative readiness, rejection of unqualified NPCs, deterministic intent,
exactly-once attempts and consequences, save-safe player attempts, single
victor, legal NPC victory, conclusion without player challenge, Chronicle
integration, Dawn privacy, resolved-world lock, migration, and eventual
termination.

======================================================================
41. PERFORMANCE
======================================================================

Resolve strategic decisions during End Day or relevant state changes, not per
frame. Continue outcome-oriented simulation and keep Android UI responsive.

======================================================================
42. OUT OF SCOPE
======================================================================

Do not add a full Main Menu, full factions, multiple Dragons, New Game+, endless
postgame, regions, cinematic branching, procedural bosses, parties, complex
quests, more sound, networking, multiplayer, or cloud saves. One good finite
season is the target.

======================================================================
43. COMPLETION STANDARD
======================================================================

LGD-009 is complete when Dragon pressure escalates coherently; residents react;
real candidates emerge; player and NPCs can become ready; NPCs can attempt and
win; the player can attempt and win; failure is coherent; no deadlock exists;
Dragon facts integrate into social/narrative systems; resolution ends ordinary
simulation; and the Final Chronicle/Season Record remain deterministic and
save-safe.

Essential experience:

THE PLAYER IS LIVING INSIDE A WORLD HEADING TOWARD A CRISIS.

Not:

THE PLAYER IS GRINDING UNTIL A BOSS BUTTON UNLOCKS.

======================================================================

======================================================================

rules, Dragon mechanics, failure consequences, resolution architecture,
